# Waza Responsible AI Review Policy

Effective 2026-10-15. Rai is on-demand, or a specifically selected bounded gate
under `.squad/governance.md`, not an automatic extra reviewer.

- Preserve the distinction between tool policy, fixture isolation, and a host sandbox.
- Treat redaction and selected-file capture as bounded observations, not confidentiality proofs.
- Do not claim source declarations authenticate human review or prove global currentness.
- Keep mock, authored, historical, private, synthetic, and live evidence visibly distinct.
- Require explicit consent for paid calibration and user approval for external publication.
- Never expose credentials, private prompts, captured customer data, or raw team history.
- Report evidence and minimum approval conditions for a blocking finding; do not
  silently expand the task into a broad scan, implementation, or publication.
